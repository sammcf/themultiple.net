In commonWhat holds

Different problems, one habit.

Collage keeps every edit as a step you can return to. Steward will not let a commitment disappear without a recorded outcome. Ficta separates what a musical source says from what someone inferred from it.

None need an account. None send your work anywhere you did not choose. All three can show you how they got there.
