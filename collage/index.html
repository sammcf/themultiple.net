Collage for iPhone and iPad

Make layered images without giving up your privacy.

Collage is a focused, local-first image compositor. Import photos, add image and text layers, refine your composition, and export a transparent PNG. Your projects stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Quick start

From photo to finished PNG.

  1. 1

    Create a project

    Tap + on the project list to open a new canvas.

  2. 2

    Choose a base

    Tap Base and select the photo that should define the canvas.

  3. 3

    Build the composition

    Use Layer and Text, then move, scale, rotate, crop, and reorder.

  4. 4

    Export

    Tap Export to preview, save to Photos, or share the finished PNG.
