CollageiPhone · iPad

Image compositor

Layered images, built on device.

Import a photo, stack image and text layers on it, transform them until the composition is right, then export one flattened PNG with its transparency intact. Projects stay on your device unless you share them.

PipelineEvery edit is a step
  1. basesets the canvas · 4096px cap
  2. layerimported, ordered
  3. textstyled, placed
  4. transformmove · scale · rotate · crop
  5. flattenPNG, alpha preserved

Every committed edit becomes a node on the app's timeline. Step back one edit at a time, or jump straight to an earlier state — nothing is discarded while it is still on the strip.
