Support

Collage is designed to keep image compositing focused and local to your device. If something is not working as expected, start here.

Contact support

Email Sam McFarlane and include the details listed below. TestFlight testers can also attach screenshots and diagnostics through TestFlight feedback.

Email sam@themultiple.net

Before reporting a problem

  1. Confirm you are using the newest Collage build available to you.
  2. Save the project, close Collage, reopen it, and try the action again.
  3. Keep the affected project and original images until the issue is understood.

What to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The Collage version and build number.
  • Clear steps that reproduce the problem.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it does not expose private content.
  • The exported PNG when the issue concerns dimensions, transparency, or visual output.

Photos access

Collage asks for Photos access only when you import an image or save an export. You can review its access in the iOS Settings app under Apps → Collage → Photos.

Projects and backups

Collage projects are stored locally on your device. Collage does not provide cloud sync or an account-based backup. Export important finished work before deleting the app or erasing the device.

Exports

Collage exports flattened PNG files. If an export looks incorrect, check it in Photos and, when possible, in a second image viewer before reporting the problem.

Privacy

Collage has no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system, or remote backend. Read the complete Collage Privacy Policy.
