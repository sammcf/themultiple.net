Collage

Privacy Policy

Collage is a local-only photo compositing app. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, cloud sync, or server-side processing.

Effective and last updated 8 August 2026

Summary

Your images and projects stay on your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

Data we collect

Collage does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. This includes contact information, identifiers, location, purchase history, browsing history, usage analytics, and user-generated content.

Photo Library permission

Collage requests photo-library access only to import photos you choose into a project and to save finished PNG exports back to Photos. Photo access is used on-device for actions you initiate.

Local storage

Collage stores the following information locally on your device so it can preserve your work:

  • Project metadata and edit history.
  • Imported image assets used by a project.
  • Generated project thumbnails.
  • Exported PNG files you choose to save or share.

Diagnostics and beta feedback

Collage does not contain an analytics or diagnostics SDK. Apple may process App Store or TestFlight diagnostics and feedback under Apple’s own terms. The developer may receive information you deliberately submit through TestFlight feedback or a support email.

Tracking

Collage does not track you across apps, websites, or services.

Third parties

Collage does not include advertising, analytics, or data-broker SDKs, and does not send project content to a third-party service.

Children’s privacy

Because Collage does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions about Collage, email sam@themultiple.net.
